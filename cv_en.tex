\section{About me}
    \justifying

I am a software engineer at \href{https://pageproof.com/}{PageProof}, a small remote team building online proofing for marketing and creative teams. My part is the integrations platform: the plugins that let people review proofs inside Canva, Microsoft Office, Adobe Creative Cloud, Final Cut Pro and Jira, the OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect server that authenticates them, and the Electron desktop app. Most of it is TypeScript, React and Node.js; Swift and C++ appear when a host application leaves no other option. \textbf{Languages:} Portuguese (native) and English (fluent).
